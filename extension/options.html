TechThrust Assistant

Your voice and chat assistant as a companion on the websites you visit. It can answer, guide you around the page and take you to other pages.

  1. In the console, open the widget you want to use and switch on Allow in the TechThrust Chrome extension, then save.
  2. Copy its widget key here, with the server address, and press Save.
  3. Use the toolbar button to open it, hide it on a site, or switch it off.
